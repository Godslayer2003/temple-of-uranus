Downloaded maps: Color, NormalGL, Roughness (Metalness/Displacement not
used). Resized from the original 1K JPGs to 512x512 and re-compressed
with ffmpeg for web weight (~40KB total for all three maps combined).

Used in static/scene.js as a real PBR gold texture (color + normal +
roughness maps) applied to pedestals and gold-toned trim, in place of a
flat MeshStandardMaterial color with no surface detail.
